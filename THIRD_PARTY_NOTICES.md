## Source links and clinical material

Clinical source links and text-only attributions are preserved in the public
reference data. A link to a paper or public formulary is a citation, not a
redistribution license for that source. Full guideline PDFs are not bundled.
Public NTUH formulary URLs may be retained after anonymous public-access checks;
private HIS endpoints and captured hospital source material remain excluded.

When adding third-party material, record its original source, author/creator,
license URL, exact figure or segment, changes and content hash. Do not infer
reuse rights from a file being downloadable or already present in another repository.
